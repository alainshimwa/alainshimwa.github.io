Kairsh

An AI copilot for customer messaging.

Year
2026
Team
Solo
Built with
JavaScript, HTML, CSS

Kairsh puts every customer conversation in one inbox. AI drafts replies, scores leads, and schedules follow-ups, so owners get more control of their business.

Decisions

AI flags, rules decide.
AI reads tone and spots sensitive topics. Fixed rules use those flags to decide what sends, and the per-minute send limit never depends on a model.
Prices expire.
Kairsh only quotes prices the owner has confirmed, and each confirmation expires after a set period. A menu parser can suggest prices but can’t set them.
Each business defines a hot lead.
At setup, AI suggests what a hot lead means for that industry, and the owner adjusts it. Someone asking to book is hot. Someone who just bought usually isn’t.
